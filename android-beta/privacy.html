ANDROID BETA SIGNUP

Your signup information

Updated September 14, 2026. This notice applies to the First Then Board Android beta signup form, separately from information stored inside the app.

What we collect

We collect your first name, the email address you use with Google Play, whether you plan to test on a phone or tablet, and an optional device model. We record the invitation source, signup time, consent version and tester-group enrollment status.

This form is for adults 18 and older. Please do not submit a child’s name, diagnosis, health information, photos or other information about a child.

How we use it

ChipTech LLC uses these details to coordinate beta access, add your Google Play email address to its tester group, understand which Android devices are being tested, and contact you about this beta. We do not sell signup information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Where it goes

The website and signup database are hosted by Cloudflare. Cloudflare Turnstile checks submissions for automated abuse, and Cloudflare processes network information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the service. Your Google Play email is shared with Google to manage tester-group membership. Accepting Google Play’s testing invitation is a separate step under Google’s terms.

Signup records are available to authorized ChipTech administrators. They are not published on the website or stored in the GitHub repository. Tester details are not shared with the clinic through this form.

Keeping and removing your information

We keep signup details for beta coordination and remove them when they are no longer needed. To correct your information, withdraw from the signup list, or request removal from the tester group, email support@chiptechllc.com using the address you registered. You can also leave the test through Google Play.

Questions? Contact support@chiptechllc.com.
