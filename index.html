02 / The studio

It started with
one family.

ChipTech LLCIndependent. Intentional. Personal.

The best reason to build an app is knowing someone who needs it.

First Then Board began at home, as a way to support our daughter through daily routines with clear visual steps. What started as a personal project became our first app.

That experience shapes how we work at ChipTech. We’re a small mobile app studio, building our own products around real needs and paying attention to the details that make them easier to use.
