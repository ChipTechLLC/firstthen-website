Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 27, 2025  ·  Last Updated: September 16, 2026

ChipTech LLC provides First Then Board for children and adult caregivers on iOS and Android. This policy describes the app's local records, online services, purchases, and support. Features and privacy controls can differ by app version and platform.

Beta registration: Adult testers who sign up on this website provide contact and device information. The separate Android beta signup privacy notice explains enrollment, Google Group membership, and beta emails.

1. Photos, Boards, Profiles, and History

The app stores your boards, selected photos, child profiles, saved routines, and completion history on your device. Creating and using a board does not upload this content to a ChipTech account. The app does not require a ChipTech account. A child profile name is a local label, not an online account.

When you select an image from a photo library or document provider, that provider may have its own cloud storage and privacy settings. Exporting or sharing history sends a copy to the destination you choose. Those exported files and original source photos are outside the app's deletion controls.

In the current Android release, live boards start without photos in a new app session; saved Quick Boards and photo-library entries persist. Android app-data cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. Backup behavior on other platforms or older versions depends on that version and your device settings.

2. Online Photo Search

Unsplash search sends your search words to our Cloudflare-hosted service, which requests results from Unsplash. Selecting a result retrieves the image from Unsplash and reports the download to Unsplash as required by its service. Downloaded images can then be reused locally.

Cloudflare and Unsplash receive network information needed to operate and protect their services, such as IP addresses and request details. Our proxy uses IP addresses for rate limiting. Please use activity words such as “breakfast” or “playground,” and do not put a child's name or other private information into a search.

See Cloudflare's privacy policy and Unsplash's privacy policy. These providers' service records and retention are governed by their own practices.

3. Advertising and Diagnostics

The free app uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. Premium removes banner ads. We do not use Apple Ads to serve these banners.

Google's advertising SDK can process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from an IP address), ad and app interactions, device or app identifiers, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Non-personalized ads still involve some data processing. We do not request precise GPS location for advertising.

The current Android release requests child-directed and under-age-of-consent treatment and G-rated ad content. Android advertising-ID access permissions are removed. These controls restrict ad behavior; they do not mean that Google receives no network or diagnostic data. Google's User Messaging Platform checks applicable privacy requirements before ads load and provides privacy options when required. The iOS app's available controls depend on the installed version.

See Google's privacy policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures. The app does not include a separate social-media tracking or general-purpose analytics SDK.

4. Purchases

Apple handles iOS purchases and Google Play handles Android purchases. The app receives purchase and entitlement information to unlock Premium, verify ownership, and restore access. ChipTech does not receive your payment-card details. Store transaction records are governed by Apple's and Google's privacy policies. Manage subscriptions through the store where you purchased them; uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

5. Children and Caregiver Controls

First Then Board is intended for all age groups, including children using visual schedules with adult support. Child profile names, board photos, and activity records are kept in the app's local storage rather than sent to ChipTech as part of board use. Online photo search and third-party advertising process the service information described above.

Adults should manage purchases, online photo searches, support messages, and exports. Do not include a child's private information or photographs in search words, feedback, or support messages. If you believe information about a child has been sent to us, contact support@chiptechllc.com so we can investigate and address the request.

6. Support and Feedback

When you send a support or feedback email, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or device details you choose to include. We use them to investigate and respond. An in-app email draft is not sent until you send it through your email application. Email is handled by the relevant email providers, including our Google Workspace service.

You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing support@chiptechllc.com. We will review the request and explain any records that must be retained for legal, security, or transaction purposes. Copies held by your email provider or other recipients are subject to their controls.

7. Retention, Deletion, and Security

Activity history is retained locally for up to 90 days. Use the app's deletion controls to remove profiles and associated records. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes its private local records. Original photos, exported CSV files, and external backups must be managed separately. ChipTech cannot remotely retrieve or delete records stored only on your device.

Advertising, store, hosting, and email providers apply their own retention policies to the information they process. We do not claim that online services retain no data. Beta registration retention is described in the separate beta notice.

The app uses HTTPS for its online search requests, and Google documents encrypted transmission for its Mobile Ads SDK. Local information relies on the protections of your device and the app. No device or online service can guarantee complete security.

8. Your Choices and Questions

You can use local photos without online search, manage available advertising privacy choices, purchase Premium to remove banner ads, control device permissions, delete local records, and choose whether to send support messages or exports. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information held about you. Contact us to make a request. We cannot access local-only app records on your behalf.

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. For privacy questions, contact ChipTech LLC at support@chiptechllc.com or visit our support page.
